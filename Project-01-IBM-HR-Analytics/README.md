# 👥 IBM HR Analytics — Employee Attrition & Performance

Interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built using Microsoft Excel to analyze employee attrition, workforce characteristics, job roles, compensation, satisfaction, and employee experience.

## 📖 Executive Summary

This project analyzes employee workforce data to identify key patterns related to employee attrition and workforce performance.

An interactive HR Analytics Dashboard was developed using Microsoft Excel, Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI Cards, and Slicers to help management understand workforce composition, attrition patterns, employee demographics, compensation, job satisfaction, and experience.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end approach to transforming employee data into meaningful HR insights and business recommendations.

## 🎯 Business Problem

- Employee attrition was impacting workforce stability.
- Management lacked a centralized view of employee attrition patterns.
- Identifying high-risk employee groups was time-consuming.
- Workforce characteristics varied across departments and job roles.
- Compensation, satisfaction, and experience needed to be analyzed together.
- Manual reporting made workforce monitoring less efficient.

## 🎯 Business Objective

Develop an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard that enables decision-makers to:

- Monitor Total Employees
- Track Employee Attrition
- Calculate Attrition Rate
- Analyze Attrition by Department
- Analyze Attrition by Job Role
- Understand Gender Distribution
- Analyze Employee Experience
- Monitor Average Age
- Analyze Average Monthly Income
- Evaluate Job Satisfaction
- Identify workforce patterns
- Support data-driven HR decisions
